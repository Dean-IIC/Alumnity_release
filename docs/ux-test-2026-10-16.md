# UX test: four people use Alumnity (16 October 2026)

Four test members walked through the site on the local copy, each in their own browser:

- a **student** (CSE, joined 2023), on a phone (390 px);
- an **alumnus** (ECE, joined 2012), on a laptop (1366 px);
- a **teacher**, invited by the desk, on a laptop;
- an **administrator** (the super administrator), on a laptop.

Each page was checked for errors, layout on its screen, and against `docs/design-system.md`
and `docs/design-research.md`. The question behind every check: *would this person
come back tomorrow?* No page threw an error; nothing ran off the side of a phone.

## What we found, and what was done

| # | Who | Found | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Teacher | **A teacher couldn't join at all.** Joining needs a line in the student register, and staff aren't in it. | Staff invitations: the desk (Team tab) invites by email with a role and department; they join at `/join/staff` with an emailed code and a password. Join and Log in point teachers there. Tested. |
| 2 | Teacher | "Dr. Test Teacher" was greeted as "Dr." everywhere (Home, emails, messages). | One first-name rule everywhere, past titles and initials ("Dr. K. Asha Rao" → "Asha"). |
| 3 | Teacher | Their page said "✓ On the college register" (untrue for staff) and showed the 1998 campus. | Staff pages say "Faculty at HKBK College of Engineering" and show the campus as it is now. |
| 4 | Student | Their page asked what they're "happy to help" with (mentoring, referring, talks): an alumnus's questions. | Students are asked what they're **looking for**: a mentor, an internship, a referral. Prompts on their page speak to a student. |
| 5 | All | Getting started was the same for everyone ("connect with your batch": teachers have none). | Steps per role: a student's ends with asking a senior; an alumnus's with mentoring and a group; staff put up an event. "Happy to mentor" is one tap, right there. |
| 6 | Alumnus, teacher | Ask a senior opened with the asking form; their own questions and the "turn on mentoring" switch were below it, and the switch sent them to edit their whole page. | Seniors see questions sent to them first, with a one-tap **I'm happy to mentor**; asking is folded away below. |
| 7 | Student | Messages and Notifications were dead ends for someone new ("open a connection"; "nothing yet"). | Messages offers connections to write to, or people to connect with; Notifications offers three things to start and people you may know. |
| 8 | Student (phone) | Causes, Groups and Events opened with their full Propose / Start / Put up forms, burying the page. | Each form is folded behind its own button and opens in place. |
| 9 | All | People, batch, connection and group lists showed names as text (the research's "faces in every list"). | Every person line starts with their photo, or their ink seal. |
| 10 | Alumnus | Lists said "ECE · 2012" (the year they joined) but their page "Batch of 2016" (the year they left). | One way everywhere: "ECE · 2012–16", as colleges write batches. |
| 11 | All | The public footer's "Play the intro" setting showed inside the app. | The app's footer keeps the links, not the intro setting. |
| 12 | Student | A lone ✓ on its own line on their page (phone). | Fixed. |
| 13 | Local only | The dev server sent a real email through the old SMTP settings in `.env.local`. | Local development never sends mail (`.env.development.local`); it prints instead. |
